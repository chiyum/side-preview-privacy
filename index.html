Side Preview 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 1 日

總結

Side Preview 是在 Chrome 側欄預覽檔案的擴充套件。所有檔案都只在你的電腦上解析，不會上傳到任何伺服器。本套件不蒐集、不儲存、不傳送任何個人資料或使用紀錄，也不含任何分析、廣告或追蹤程式。

套件會處理哪些資料

  • 你要預覽的檔案：套件從檔案所在的網站（使用你在該網站的登入狀態）或你拖放的本機檔案取得內容，只在瀏覽器記憶體中解析與顯示。關閉該預覽分頁或側欄後即釋放。檔案內容不會傳送給開發者或任何第三方。
  • 下載改為預覽：只有在你開啟這個開關後，網頁要下載 PDF、圖片、影片或試算表時，套件會讀取該筆下載的網址，並以你的登入狀態重新抓取一次以在側欄顯示（不支援的格式照常下載）。下載網址與內容同樣只在你的電腦上處理，不會傳送給任何人。
  • 設定：「下載改為預覽」開關、已啟用後台模式的網站清單（只有網站位址，例如 https://example.com），存在你電腦上的 Chrome 擴充儲存空間，不會同步或上傳。
  • 後台模式的登入通行證：只有在你對某個網站按下「啟用後台模式」並同意後，套件才會在該網站觀察網頁自己發出的請求，記下登入通行證（例如 Authorization header），用來抓你要預覽的檔案。通行證只存在記憶體（chrome.storage.session），關閉瀏覽器即自動清除；你也可以隨時在設定中移除該網站，通行證會同時刪除。未啟用的網站，套件完全不會讀取其請求。

不做的事

  • 不蒐集姓名、電子郵件、瀏覽紀錄、位置或任何可識別個人的資訊。
  • 不把任何資料傳送到開發者或第三方伺服器；套件沒有自己的伺服器。
  • 不販售、出租或轉移任何資料。
  • 不從遠端載入任何程式碼。

權限說明

套件要求的每項權限都只用於預覽你選擇的檔案：側欄顯示（sidePanel）、右鍵選單（contextMenus）、在原網頁取得檔案（scripting、所有網站存取權限）、保存設定與記憶體中的通行證（storage）、後台模式觀察已同意網站的請求（webRequest）、下載改為預覽與儲存檔案（downloads）。

Side Preview Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Summary

Side Preview is a Chrome extension that previews files in the side panel. All files are parsed only on your computer and are never uploaded to any server. The extension does not collect, store or transmit any personal data or usage data, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Data the extension handles

  • Files you preview: fetched from the site hosting them (using your existing login on that site) or from files you drag in, and parsed only in browser memory. They are released when you close the preview tab or the side panel, and are never sent to the developer or any third party.
  • Preview downloads: only when you turn this switch on, when a page tries to download a PDF, image, video or spreadsheet, the extension reads that download's URL and fetches it once more with your existing login to display it in the side panel (other formats download normally). The URL and content are processed only on your computer and never sent to anyone.
  • Settings: the "Preview downloads" switch and the list of sites with back-office mode enabled (site addresses only, e.g. https://example.com), stored in Chrome's extension storage on your computer. Not synced or uploaded.
  • Back-office mode credentials: only after you enable back-office mode for a site and consent, the extension observes that site's own requests and remembers its login credential (e.g. the Authorization header) to fetch files you choose to preview. Credentials are kept in memory only (chrome.storage.session) and cleared automatically when you close the browser; removing the site in Settings deletes them immediately. Requests of sites you have not enabled are never read.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not collect names, email addresses, browsing history, location or any personally identifiable information.
  • It does not send any data to the developer or third-party servers; the extension has no server of its own.
  • It does not sell, rent or transfer any data.
  • It does not load any remote code.

Permissions

Every permission is used only to preview files you choose: side panel display (sidePanel), right-click menu (contextMenus), fetching files from the original page (scripting, access to all sites), settings and in-memory credentials (storage), observing requests on consented sites in back-office mode (webRequest), and download-to-preview and saving files (downloads).
